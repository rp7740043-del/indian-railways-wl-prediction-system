How WL Predictor Works

A transparent, beginner-friendly system designed to take the guesswork out of waitlisted train journeys.

1

1. Enter Details

Input your train number, travel class (SL, 3A, 2A, 1A, CC), quota, current waitlist number, and days left before travel.

2

2. See Chance

Get an instant confirmation probability (High 70%+, Medium 40-69%, Low below 40%), visual progress bar, and formula breakdown.

3

3. Track Daily

Log daily status changes like WL 35 → WL 24 → WL 15 on an interactive timeline and watch the clearance curve bend toward RAC.
