About WL Predictor

How the mathematical prediction model works and key railway reservation rules.

Explainable Probability Formula

Instead of opaque machine learning black-boxes, WL Predictor relies on an explainable 6-pillar algorithm mirroring real Indian Railway berth dynamics.

Starting from a neutral 50% baseline, adjustments are made for current queue depth, remaining days for passenger dropouts, recorded daily clearance velocity, coach class capacity, and quota cancellation rules.

High (70%+ Chance)

Strong likelihood of reaching RAC or Confirmed before final charting.

Medium (40% - 69%)

Moderate chance. Highly dependent on last-minute chart preparation releases.

Low (Below 40%)

Minimal confirmation chance. Alternative travel arrangements recommended.
